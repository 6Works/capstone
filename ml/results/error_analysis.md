## 2. Corpus & Residual Error Insights

1. **`MINDS-14` Crowd Reference Noise vs. `Svarah N=1,000`:** On `MINDS-14 (498)`, both CPU `whisper-small.en` (`0.2508`) and Kaggle T4 `whisper-large-v3-turbo` (`0.2517`, `CER=0.2013`) hit an identical `~25%` WER floor caused by ASR-generated reference labels (`"pay a bail"`). On clean human `en-IN` clips (`ai4bharat/Svarah`, `N=1,000`), `whisper-large-v3-turbo` cut WER from `0.1521` to **`0.0888`** (`p < 0.001`).
2. **ITN Formatting Penalties (`qa_18`, `pn_12`, `pn_23`):** Verbatim strings (`"Calculate 95-28% error reduction."` vs `"95 minus 28 percent"`) inflated top `small_improved` residual WER (`0.5714`). Added [`compute_itn_wer`](../metrics/eval_metrics.py) for fair dual-track reporting.
3. **E2E Stage-C Short-Fragment Drop & Turn Latency (`e2e_ablation_matrix.json` v1):** Replies shorter than `<2.5 s` (`qa_10`, `en-GB_388_5`) and `12` short-reference `<5 s` cloning prompts triggered variance (`CAM++ < 0.50` / `short_ref_warnings`), while sequential REST turn latency stays `3.59–6.87 s` until streaming WebSocket + chunked TTS lands in `v2.0`.
