Log-Mel Spectrogram Feature Extraction

Raw waveform samples $x[n]$ are non-stationary. To extract speech phonemes, the input is converted into time-frequency representation via Short-Time Fourier Transform (STFT):

Mel Scale Frequency Warp:
m = 2595 · log10(1 + f / 700)

Human hearing exhibits non-linear frequency resolution (high sensitivity below 1 kHz, logarithmic spacing above). Applying 80 triangular Mel filterbanks aligns feature density with human auditory perception.

Standard Feature Window Specifications:
  • Window Size: 25 milliseconds (400 samples at 16 kHz) with Hann tapering.
  • Hop Length: 10 milliseconds (160 samples), giving 100 frames/sec.
  • Filterbanks: 80 mel-frequency channels spanning 0 Hz to 8000 Hz.

Whisper Encoder-Decoder Attention Architecture

Whisper uses a Transformer seq2seq architecture. Audio frames are encoded into continuous representations, while an autoregressive text decoder predicts BPE tokens:

Scaled Dot-Product Cross-Attention:
Attention(Q, K, V) = softmax(Q · K^T / sqrt(d_k)) · V

Queries (Q) from the text decoder attend to Keys (K) and Values (V) from the 80-channel audio spectrogram encoder, achieving robust alignment under ambient noise.

faster-whisper CTranslate2 Optimization:
  • INT8 Quantization: Reduces weight memory by 4x with zero perplexity degradation.
  • Silero VAD: Filters 100% of silence frames before Transformer decoding.
  • Latency: Decreased from 1200ms to < 250ms on standard CPUs.
