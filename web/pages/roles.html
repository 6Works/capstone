Decoupled Multi-Tier Architecture & Psychological Flow

Psychological Clarity & Focus

Separating persona selection (Tier 1) from the operational sub-mode configuration (Tier 2) ensures candidates or interviewers focus with serious intent without UI clutter.

Single Responsibility (SRP)

/roles owns Persona identity cataloging; /interviewer-mode owns sub-mode operational switching and live interview telemetry.

Open/Closed Principle (OCP)

New personas and multi-tier sub-modes register seamlessly without touching existing speech, ASR, or TTS microservices.
