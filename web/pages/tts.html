Vocal Tract Physics (Chapter 2)

Human speech generation relies on the source-filter model:

1. Vocal Fold Excitation (Source):

Airflow vibrates vocal folds at fundamental frequency F0, producing harmonic comb spectrum.

2. Vocal Tract Resonances (Filter):

Pharynx, oral and nasal cavities shape formant bands (F1, F2, F3) creating distinctive vowels and timbre.

Psychoacoustic Perception (Chapter 2)

Human auditory perception compresses frequency resolution logarithmically:

Mel-Scale Filterbanks:

Triangular overlapping filters mimicking the cochlea's non-linear critical bands, prioritizing 300Hz-3kHz.

Acoustic Cadence & Breaths:

Conversational speech requires 150-250ms breathing pauses at clause boundaries to avoid auditory fatigue.
