Acoustic Cadence & Breathing Mechanics

Traditional TTS sounds robotic because synthetic engines speak at a flat, unvarying tempo. Human conversational speech is punctuated by physiological and cognitive pauses:

Clause & Comma Micro-Pauses (120 - 220 ms):

Short vocal tract relaxations allowing listeners to process preceding cognitive phrases before the subsequent clause begins.

Sentence Boundaries & Inhalations (300 - 550 ms):

Full stops and semicolons require complete vocal fold abduction for lung replenishment. Omitting this causes auditory fatigue.

The add_human_pauses() Transformation:

Our decoupled service transforms plain text strings by inserting phonetic breath markers and calibrated punctuation ellipses before passing to the vocoder.

ITTSService Interface & Data Transfer Object 

class ITTSService(ABC):
    @abstractmethod
    def synthesize(self, text: str, **kwargs) -> TTSResult:
        """Synthesizes text into acoustic waveform and persists output."""
        pass

class TTSResult(BaseModel):
    file_path: str
    audio_format: str = "wav"
    duration_sec: float = 0.0
    sample_rate: int = 16000
    provider: str = "pyttsx3"
    metadata: Dict[str, Any] = Field(default_factory=dict)
