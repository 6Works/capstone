Mode A

Autonomous Solo Interviewer

Candidate ⮂ AI Speech-to-Speech Dialogue

Mira assumes the persona of a senior hiring manager conducting the full interview autonomously. She speaks vocal questions, analyzes candidate responses, gives verbal feedback, adjusts question difficulty dynamically, and generates an end-of-interview scorecard.

Speech Output: Full natural voice synthesis with pauses (Stage 5 TTS active).
Interaction: Candidate converses directly with Mira via microphone.
Deliverable: Real-time competency grading & scorecard.

Mode B

Human Interviewer Co-Pilot & Assistant

Candidate ➔ AI Silent HUD ➔ Human Interviewer

Designed specifically for an actual human interviewer conducting an interview in real-time. Mira operates silently in the background, transcribing candidate answers, feeding the human interviewer 3 instant follow-up probe questions, verifying claims, and drafting notes.

Speech Output: Strictly muted (silent augmented intelligence HUD).
Live Guidance: 3 clickable follow-up probes generated per candidate response.
Deliverable: Real-time technical fact-checking & auto-compiled interview notes.
